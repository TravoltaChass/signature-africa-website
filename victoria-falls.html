Feel The Rush

Adrenaline & Adventure

The Batoka Gorge is one of the world's great adventure playgrounds, Grade 5 rapids, a 111m gorge swing, and Africa's highest bungee jump.

Victoria Falls Bungee Jump

111 metres of freefall from the historic Victoria Falls Bridge, suspended between Zimbabwe and Zambia.

Bridge Swing & Bridge Slide

A gentler alternative to the bungee, with the same spectacular gorge and bridge views.

Gorge Swing & Zip Line

Freefall into the Batoka Gorge on the swing, or fly across it at speed on the zip line.

White Water Rafting (Batoka Gorge)

Half or full day rafting through Grade 3–5 rapids on one of the world's premier white water rivers.
