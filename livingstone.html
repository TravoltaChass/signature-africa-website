Devil's Pool & Livingstone Island

A once-in-a-lifetime swim at the very lip of Victoria Falls, only accessible from the Zambian side.

Livingstone Island Morning Breeze Tour

An early boat transfer to Livingstone Island with breakfast, before the day's crowds arrive.

Livingstone Island High Tea / Lunch & Devil's Pool Swim

A guided swim in Devil's Pool followed by high tea or a plated lunch overlooking the Falls.

Speedboat Transfer & Dining Package
