Kasane, Botswana

Gateway to Chobe National Park, VIP airport handling and unforgettable game-viewing on the Chobe River.

Botswana

Where The Rivers Meet The Wild

Kasane sits at the confluence of the Chobe and Zambezi rivers, and at the entrance to Chobe National Park, home to one of Africa's largest elephant populations. We handle every arrival detail and the game-viewing that follows.
